# 🚗 Automobile Sales Recession Analysis



## 🧩 Problem Statement

Economic recessions can significantly impact consumer spending and purchasing behavior. The automotive industry is particularly sensitive to economic downturns due to changes in income levels, unemployment rates, and consumer confidence.



This project analyzes historical automobile sales data to understand how recession periods affect vehicle sales, advertising expenditures, and market performance across different vehicle categories.



The project includes both exploratory data analysis and the development of an interactive dashboard to support business decision-making.



---



## 📊 Dataset

- **Name:** Historical Automobile Sales Dataset

- **Source:** [IBM Data Science Professional Certificate Lab Dataset](https://cf-courses-data.s3.us.cloud-object-storage.appdomain.cloud/d51iMGfp_t0QpO30Lym-dw/automobile-sales.csv)

- **Format:** CSV

## ⚙️ Methodology



### 1. Data Exploration

- Dataset inspection and validation

- Statistical summary generation

- Missing value verification

- Identification of recession and non-recession periods



### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

- Annual automobile sales trends

- Recession versus non-recession comparisons

- Vehicle category performance analysis

- Seasonal sales behavior evaluation

- Advertising expenditure analysis



### 3. Economic Impact Assessment

- Analysis of unemployment rate effects on sales

- Evaluation of consumer confidence indicators

- Identification of recession-driven purchasing patterns



### 4. Dashboard Development

- Interactive dashboard creation using Plotly Dash

- Dynamic filtering by report type and year

- Interactive visualizations for business insights



---



## 📊 Key Insights

- Automobile sales decline significantly during recession periods.

- Certain vehicle categories demonstrate greater resilience during economic downturns.

- Advertising expenditure patterns shift during recessions as companies adjust marketing strategies.

- Higher unemployment rates are associated with lower automobile sales.

- Economic conditions strongly influence consumer purchasing behavior within the automotive sector.
